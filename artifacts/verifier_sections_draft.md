## §5.4 Limitations and honesty

Verifier numbers are public-eval diagnostics; labels are used only for scoring, never inside the search. The engine is a research module, not a submitted competition entry. An empty acceptance set is an impossibility certificate for the bounded DSL/parameter class, not a proof over all programs. Depth-3 was measured only on the 20-task dev20/joint subset (0/20 certified) and is not extrapolated to eval120. LLM nulls are compute-limited (token-cap truncation), hence infrastructure nulls, not capability evidence. No hidden-test or state-of-the-art claim is made.
